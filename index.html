Privacy Policy

Shrink Run for iPhone and iPad · Last updated October 28, 2026

Shrink Run is a game you can play without an account. We don't ask for your name, email address, or location, and we don't run our own analytics or tracking. This page explains the limited data that is used when you play.

Your progress

The game saves your best score, coins, gems, unlocked skins and worlds, settings, and purchase status on your device. This information stays on your device until you delete the app.

Apple services

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is synced between your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This data is handled by Apple and is not accessible to us.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your best score and achievements to Apple. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment details.

Advertising

Shrink Run shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and ad interaction and usage data. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Ads appear only between rounds, never during gameplay. Purchasing Remove Ads or the Starter Pack removes these ads.

In regions where required, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are asked for your consent before ads that use your data are shown.

Your choices

You can limit ad personalization in your device's iOS settings, and you can change any consent choice you made in the game when it is offered. iCloud and Game Center data can be managed or deleted in your Apple account settings. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Shrink Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
